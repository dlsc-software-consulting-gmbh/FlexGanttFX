Available Licenses

This page lists the license types available for FlexGanttFX. To purchase them simply contact us via email and we will send you a quote. Once we have received your order we will send you a license key which can be set via the FlexGanttFX API.

Maintenance Plan

A maintenance plan for FlexGanttFX is also available. The cost for this is 20% of the total licensing volume per year. Subscribers of the maintenance plan will receive premium support and free upgrades whenever a new major version has been released. Without a maintenance plan licensees will only be eligible for bug fix releases.
